# Quotas implementation outline

Non-binding, part of TASK-25. It orders the work; the ADRs decide. Numbers refer to proposed ADR-0260 to ADR-0270 in
`docs/adr/`. Nothing here starts before the architect accepts the ADRs a task depends on, and every task ends with
`make test` passing from a clean checkout and the full CI green on its branch. The tasks are cut to comparable size
(each a few thousand lines of code and tests, by estimate) so that each has a visible result on its own.

**Dependencies outside this plan.** Stage 7's rooms (built: `/config/rooms`, `room`, the panel's launches), stage 6's
broker and `tmpd` per shell (built). The network tasks N1 to N6 (`../network/plan.md`) for every part that touches
`netd`. The format of `zoraya-fs` changes in Q5 (volumes are rebuilt with `zoraya-mkfs` until a format is declared
stable).

**Order and risk.** Q1 carries the most risk (every allocation site) and everything depends on it, so it goes first.
Q5 depends on nothing else and can run in parallel with Q1 to Q4 in another worktree. Q2 and Q4 are kernel work on
Q1's counters; Q2 first, since the fork bomb is the measured harm and its teardown is the largest stall. Q6 is cut
in two (the keeper and the line, then the display), since together it was the largest task. Q3's `fsd` part needs only Q1; its `netd` part
interleaves with the network.

**Interleaving with N1 to N6.** N1 and N2 touch nothing here. N3 defines `connect`: if Q3's change (the client passes
its stream ends) is accepted before N3 starts, N3 builds that shape directly and Q3's `netd` part shrinks to the
per-scope account; otherwise Q3 changes it after N3 and before N4 builds `libposix`'s sockets on it. N6's
"per-scope connection bound ... as the shape ADR-0056's accounts will fill" *is* Q3's `netd` account: do them as one
task if both are ready, else N6 builds the bound and Q3 renames it an account. Q7's `+net` cases wait for N6; without
it, untrusted rooms may open with no `+net` words allowed (ADR-0268's open question). A workable order: Q1, then Q2 and
Q5 in parallel with N1 and N2; Q3's `fsd` part and Q4 with N3; Q3's `netd` part with N4 or N6; Q6a; Q6b; Q7 (first stage without `+net`, second after N6).

**What each task measures,** for `docs/design.md` and its report: Q1 the charge's cost on `portbench` and a channel
round trip, and the kernel reserve against the heap's overhead; Q2 the fork bomb's time to its limit and the longest
masked interval before and after; Q3 each service's worst-case size against the table; Q4 the terminal's key latency
next to capped spinners, at 20 ms, 10 ms and 100 ms periods; Q5 a growing request's cost with a limit three levels up; Q6a the keeper's round trip per launch; Q6b the
panel's reading cost per second; Q7 the gate's run time on CI.

**Deliberately not in this plan:** shares (weights) between jobs, a burst allowance, lending CPU to services,
donated session quota (Genode's form), a memory-pressure signal, disk and network bandwidth limits, limits per
command line, soft disk limits with grace periods. Each has a revisit trigger in its ADR.
