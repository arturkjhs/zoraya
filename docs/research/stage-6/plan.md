# Stage 6 outline

Non-binding, part of TASK-15. It orders the work; the ADRs decide. Numbers refer to proposed ADRs 0100 to 0110 in
`docs/adr/`. Nothing here starts before the architect accepts the ADRs it depends on, and every task ends with
`make test` passing from a clean checkout. The tasks are cut to comparable size (each a few thousand lines of code and
tests, by estimate) and so that each has a visible result on its own.

| Task | Work | Depends on | Done when | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6a | **Kernel objects.** Byte streams (`stream_create`, `stream_read`, `stream_write`, two end types, signals on both ends, `zoraya-stream` crate); `snapshot_process` with its flat buffer, move list, `self_slot`, generation rule and `Duplicable` marks in `OBJECT_TYPES`; `image_create`; `job_suspend`/`job_resume`; the program header table in the startup block; `object_get_signals` for `WNOHANG`. Order of risk: 6b and 6d carry the unknowns the ADRs name as open questions; 6a is kernel work on known ground;
6c is the largest in lines. 6b can run in parallel with 6a, since neither needs the other, and 6c needs both. The
first thing 6c and 6d each do is a host test of their protocol additions, before any guest code.

What each task measures on the way, for `docs/design.md` and the task report: 6a the copy time of a 1 MiB snapshot
and the frames it charges under HVF and TCG, and the stream transfer rate; 6c the `share` and `seek` round trip and
the resolver's cost per `open`; 6d the broker's `spawn_prepare`/`commit`/`reap` round trips and the time of an `exec`;
6e everything ADR-0110 item 11 lists, and the W4 figures for the T2 decision.

What is deliberately not in stage 6: T2's rows and W4 as a test; `tcc -run`; exception channels; `SIGTTOU` and
`TOSTOP`; an image cache; copy-on-write for the snapshot; a Rust `std` target for Zoraya; vendoring the ports'
sources. Each has a revisit trigger in its ADR.
