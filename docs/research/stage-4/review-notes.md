# Independent review of the TASK-10 ADRs

Reviewer: a subagent that did not write ADR-0050 to ADR-0055. Reviewed on branch `adr/stage-4-design` at 1742476, on 2026-11-01. ADR-0040 to ADR-0046 were read from `kernel/stage-3`, where they are proposed. Every URL in `sources.md` was fetched in this review, and `fuchsia.io` and QEMU's `hw/arm/virt.c` as well. The Disposition column is for the author.

Severity: **error** means the design as written contradicts itself or an accepted ADR, cannot be built, or states something false. **imprecision** means it is underspecified, overclaims, or leaves a tension unlisted. **note** is minor.

## Two questions asked specifically

**Soundness of the no-cycle rule (ADR-0050).** Restricted to endpoints, the rule is sound. Let an edge F→E mean "E is queued for reading on F". A new edge F→E is added only when E has no outgoing edge, because E has no handles queued, and E≠F by the self-or-peer rule. A path from E back to F would need an outgoing edge from E, so the new edge cannot close a cycle. This holds whatever the order, including handles that arrive on an endpoint already in flight, and for several handles in one message. The ADR's "last edge" sketch is therefore right, and refusing to send through the endpoint itself is not needed for this proof (only through the peer, since that makes the self-loop).

The conclusions drawn from it go too far. The rule does not bound depth or total memory (findings 2, 3). The graph leaves out other holders of handles, so INV-0050-2 as worded is false (finding 4). It does not settle `fork` (finding 1). A peer controls whether the rule refuses (finding 24).

**Driver death and `Failed` (ADR-0054, ADR-0055 against ADR-0010, ADR-0015).** Reasoning "unknown outcome, so treat as a failed flush" is a fair reading of ADR-0015 rule 6 for a request in flight. Remounting after it keeps ADR-0010 rule 3, given one thing not in the ADR: `Volume::mount` flushes first (`crates/zoraya-fs/src/volume.rs`, the comment in `mount`), so a root record shown by a device cache that outlived the driver is made stable before its freed blocks are reused. The claim fails in four other places. With no request in flight the engine cannot enter `Failed` at all (finding 9). A driver or service death is not a power cut, because the device is still running old writes (finding 10). The driver cannot be restarted as specified (finding 5). ADR-0010's rule that reads continue, and its list of `Failed` triggers, are not reconciled (finding 15, tensions table).
