4. Resolved every `#section-slug` against the actual headings (GitHub slug rules) of the cited file.
5. Queried `task-02/libc-surface.csv` (637 rows, `tier` column), `task-02/syscall-surface.csv` (68 rows) and `task-02b/syscall-delta.csv` (868 rows) with a script for every symbol, syscall and count the ADRs quote.
6. For each invariant, asked whether the stated check is an observable property a test, lint, CI script or `abi` type could decide, and whether it contradicts another rule in the batch.

Severity: **error** = claim contradicts the source, or two rules in the batch contradict each other; **imprecision** = right in substance, wrong in detail or attribution; **note** = labelling, wording, or a steelman that could be stronger.

M4 (`#m4-validation-patches`) says: "`posix_spawn` attributes cover `SETPGROUP` and `SETSIGDEF` but not handing over the terminal." GR-005 lists terminal attributes and namespace entry, not process groups. | R-0003-4 | c/other | Rule 3 vs ADR-0006 "Each POSIX process has one port" | `snapshot_process` gives the child "duplicates of every duplicable handle".
